# About

I'm a Senior Category Analyst at Parachute Health, where I do product operations work for the platform's medical supply catalog. Parachute connects healthcare facilities with the suppliers who fulfill equipment and supply orders for patients. I own the Diabetes category, about $8M in platform revenue, and before that I owned Pregnancy and Maternity.

Day to day I sit between three groups. Clinical users tell me how ordering actually works for them. Product and engineering need that turned into requirements with the edge cases spelled out. Leadership needs to know what's working and what to build next. A lot of my job is translating between them, then staying with a feature after it ships to see what the rollout teaches us.

Before Parachute I was at Global Industrial, first as a product management intern and then as a Product Operations Specialist. There I kept pricing, attribute, and vendor data accurate across a large product catalog and coordinated launches and content updates with marketing, sales, and category teams. I studied Operations Management at the University of Delaware, with a minor in Management Information Systems.

## How I work

- **I look at the workflow before the dashboard.** A number tells me where to look. The reason it's moving (or not) is usually in how people actually place orders.
- **I write things down and make people react to them.** A written rule with the edge cases listed gets corrected much faster than a conversation does.
- **I build my own tools.** When a problem is too big to do by hand, I'd rather build something that solves it than accept it as unsolvable. Then I check that the tool is actually right.
- **I stay with things after launch.** The rollout is where the real requirements show up.

## What I'm looking for

A role in product operations, strategy & operations, or product management where I can keep doing the parts of this job I like most: finding the real problem in the data, working closely with engineering to fix it, and building tools that make a team faster. I'm most interested in healthtech, AI-native companies, and other teams building product where the details of an operational workflow really matter.
