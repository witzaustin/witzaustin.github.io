# Austin Witz

I work in product operations at Parachute Health, a platform that healthcare facilities use to order medical equipment and supplies for their patients. My job sits between clinical ordering workflows and the product and engineering teams that build for them.

My work falls into three areas:

- **Feature ownership.** Own catalog and ordering features after launch, partnering with product, engineering, and customer success to iterate on them.
- **Catalog and coverage accuracy.** Maintain the product catalog and the YAML-based coverage criteria that determine whether a patient qualifies for a product, keeping both current as products and payer rules change.
- **AI and automation.** Build AI tools, workflows, and automations with Claude, Notion, and N8N.

I'm based in the New York area and looking for my next role in product operations, strategy and operations, or product management.
