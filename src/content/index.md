# Austin Witz

I work in product operations at Parachute Health, a platform that healthcare facilities use to order medical equipment and supplies for their patients. My job sits between clinical ordering workflows and the product and engineering teams that build for them.

Most of what I do falls into three buckets. I own features after they launch and keep improving them. I use SQL to find where orders are getting lost and what it would take to win them back. And I build AI tools with Claude, Gemini, and N8N that take on catalog and data quality work that used to be done by hand.

I'm based in the New York area and looking for my next role in product operations, strategy and operations, or product management.
